
ABC TECHNOLOGIES
COMPANY EXPENSE MANAGEMENT POLICY

1. HOTEL ACCOMMODATION

Employees can claim a maximum of Rs. 2000
per night for hotel accommodation during
official business trips.

2. FOOD EXPENSES

Employees can claim a maximum of Rs. 500
per day for food expenses during official travel.

3. TRANSPORTATION

Employees can claim reasonable transportation
expenses for official company activities.

4. TRAVEL EXPENSES

Employees must submit valid travel bills
and receipts for reimbursement.

5. OFFICE SUPPLIES

Office supply purchases must be related
to official company activities.

6. SOFTWARE SUBSCRIPTIONS

Software subscriptions must be approved
by the department manager.

7. DOCUMENTATION

Employees must submit valid bills,
receipts and supporting documents
for all reimbursement claims.

8. APPROVAL

All expense reimbursement claims are
subject to verification and approval
by the finance department.

9. UNAUTHORIZED EXPENSES

Personal purchases and expenses unrelated
to official company activities are not
eligible for reimbursement.

10. POLICY COMPLIANCE

Employees must follow the company expense
limits and submit accurate expense details.
